How it works

One wallet, three simple moves

UPAHAAR is built so anyone — including the elderly — can manage their health records without friction.

01

Store everything

Snap a prescription and OCR converts it to text automatically. Reports, vaccinations, allergies and family history — all in one secure wallet.

02

Understand instantly

AI summarizes your whole history into a readable clinical timeline — medicines, dosages and trends — with the original text always one tap away.

03

Share with doctors

Send your profile to any doctor with one tap. You control access, you see every request, and you can revoke it at any moment.

Security first

Your records are yours. Only yours.

Health data is the most personal data there is. UPAHAAR is designed so every access is authorized, every login is protected, and every share is reversible.

AES-grade encryption Consent-based sharing Full access logs

Two-factor authentication

Time-based one-time codes on every login — your wallet stays locked to you.

Emergency facial recognition

In a crisis, a verified medical team can bypass OTP login via face recognition to reach your timeline — with a notification sent to you immediately.

Revocable doctor access

Every doctor request appears as a security alert. Acknowledge it or revoke it — and you're notified the moment anyone opens your profile.
